# Slow Practices

Slow Practices is an idea for [handwritten.blog](https://handwritten.blog/): a shared invitation each month to slow down, pay attention, and make something by hand.

## How it works

1. An operator chooses a practice and writes a short invitation.
2. People try it in their own time and make a handwritten page about what they notice.
3. They publish it on their own blog and add it to the practice's collection, where everyone can browse the responses.

A practice could be listening to a song, taking a slow walk, or returning to the same view each day.

Next month brings a new practice. Earlier invitations and responses stay in the archive.
