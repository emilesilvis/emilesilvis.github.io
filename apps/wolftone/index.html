Your commission

A craftsperson’s job. A mystery in the work.

The guild does not need a hero. It needs someone who can read grain, hear a bad interval, and finish what another pair of hands began.

The vanished master left no confession—only a locked workshop, a ledger of increasingly improper commissions, and instruments whose interiors are larger than their cases. Each solution restores a little of the record. Each new mechanism makes the questions worse.

Office of Measures · sealed

To the journeyman appointed in settlement:

Inventory the premises. Complete all contracted work. Preserve anything that can still be made to ring true. Report, in particular, any recurrence of the fault described as a wolf tone.

By order of the Guild.

Inside the instrument

Build a program you can watch perform.

01 · Read the score

Every commission has several performances.

The starting notes change. The target halls change. Your one machine must ring true for every case.

02 · Fit the mechanism

Place parts. Draw strings. Use distance as time.

A mould appends. A damper removes. Forks read, splitters divide, couplings make two voices decide each other’s path.

03 · Hear it run

Notes travel, mechanisms move, the workshop answers.

Watch each word flow through your instrument—or advance one exact tick at a time until the fault reveals itself.
