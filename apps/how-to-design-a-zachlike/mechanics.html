A · Mechanics catalog 100 rows · M001–M135

The taxonomy behind §2, grouped into nine families. A separate row exists only when a rule changes decisions, prediction, failure, feedback, or optimization.

Ratings and game examples are editorial prompts, not measurements or proof of attribution. Inspect the cited game before borrowing a row, then test the mechanic against the five questions in §2.

Emergence scale. Low — outcomes follow directly from authored actions. Medium — several rules compose into interactions that require simulation or planning. High — global behaviour or failure modes arise from local rules, shared state, capacity, concurrency, topology, or feedback.
