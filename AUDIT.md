1. **Repair article reflow first.** On a 390-pixel viewport, the generated page widths are 679 pixels for “How much time I've spent on Math Academy,” 554 for the systems-thinking resource list, 472 for part 4 of the computer series, and 448 for part 5. This expands the surrounding paragraphs and, in the Math Academy case, the bio/navigation panel too. A reader must scroll sideways to read ordinary prose. The Math Academy page also overflows at 768 and 1024 pixels. See the [phone screenshot](/tmp/emilesilvis-deep-audit-2026-09-07/how-much-time-ive-spent-on-math-academy-390.png).

   Grid items retain their intrinsic minimum width, long content does not consistently wrap, and tables have no scroll container. Start with [the layout rules](/Users/emilesilvis/orca/workspaces/emilesilvis.github.io/trout/static/css/style.css:104) and [table styles](/Users/emilesilvis/orca/workspaces/emilesilvis.github.io/trout/static/css/style.css:170): allow the content track and item to shrink, wrap long prose links, and put wide tables/code in bounded horizontal scroll containers. Acceptance: ordinary prose remains within the viewport at 320 and 390 pixels; any necessary horizontal scrolling stays within the table or code block. This matches the distinction in [W3C's reflow guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html).
